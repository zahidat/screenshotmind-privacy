ScreenshotMind – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short

ScreenshotMind keeps your screenshots, the text found in them and your ratings on your phone. It has no account, no ads from ad networks and no tracking. We never see your screenshots.

What the app does

ScreenshotMind notices when you take a screenshot, asks you how important it is (1 to 5 stars), reads the text in the picture so you can search it, and reminds you about it until you tap Done.

What it uses, and why

  • Photos permission: to notice new screenshots, show their preview and read their text. The app only looks at images whose name contains "screenshot".
  • Notifications permission: to ask for your rating and to send reminders.
  • Background service and "run at startup": to keep noticing new screenshots, also after the phone restarts. You can pause this any time in Settings.

What is stored

Your star ratings, the text read from each screenshot and your settings are saved inside the app on your phone. Tapping Done removes a screenshot from the app (the picture itself is never touched or deleted). Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

What is sent anywhere

Your screenshots, their text and your ratings are never sent to us or to anyone else. Text is read on the phone itself.

The text-reading part of the app (Google ML Kit) may send anonymous technical information to Google, such as the phone model, Android version, app version and how fast the reading was. It does not include your pictures or their text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

The ad card

The card at the bottom of the home screen is a promotion for the developer's own web design service. It is not served by an ad network and does not track you. If you tap it, your phone's web browser opens zahid.at. That website has its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can pause tracking or turn off quiet hours in Settings, remove photo or notification access in your phone's app settings, tap Done to forget a screenshot, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this website.
